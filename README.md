# EAD_Big_Data_Analysis

## Project Overview

This notebook performs an exploratory data analysis on the Sample Superstore sales data. Key steps include:

*   **Data Loading and Initial Inspection**: The `samplesuperstore.csv` dataset is loaded, and its structure, columns, and basic statistics are examined.
*   **Data Cleaning**: Duplicates are removed, and 'Order Date' and 'Ship Date' columns are converted to datetime objects. A 'Delivery Days' feature is engineered.
*   **Sales Performance Analysis**: Visualizations and aggregations are performed to understand:
    *   Total Sales by Region
    *   Top 10 Products by Sales
    *   Profit Distribution by Product Category
    *   Sales by Customer Segment
    *   Average Delivery Days by Ship Mode
    *   Overall Yearly Sales Trends
